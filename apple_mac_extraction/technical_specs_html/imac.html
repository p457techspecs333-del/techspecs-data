    Connections and Expansion

    M6 with 10‑core CPU

    Two Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports with support for:

    • Thunderbolt 4 (up to 40Gb/s)

    • USB 3.1 Gen 2 (up to 10Gb/s)
    • DisplayPort
    • 3.5mm headphone jack with advanced support for high‑impedance headphones
    • Configurable with 2.5Gb Ethernet

    Four Thunderbolt 4 ports with support for:

    • USB 3.1 Gen 2 (up to 10Gb/s)
    • DisplayPort
    • 3.5mm headphone jack with advanced support for high‑impedance headphones
    • 2.5Gb Ethernet

    iMac and the Environment

    Progress towards Apple 2030

    • Apple 2030 is our goal to be carbon neutral across our value chain. We design our products to be less carbon intensive by prioritising the use of recycled and renewable content and low‑carbon materials while focusing on the energy efficiency of our software and hardware.

    See Apple’s commitment

    See the iMac Product Environmen­tal Report (PDF)

    Materials

    • iMac is made with 15% recycled content,7 including:
    • 100% recycled aluminium in the stand
    • 50% recycled glass in the standard glass8
    • 100% recycled gold plating in all Apple‑designed printed circuit boards
    • 50% recycled plastic in multiple components8
    • 100% recycled rare earth elements in all magnets
    • 100% recycled tin solder in all Apple‑designed printed circuit boards

    Packaging

    • 100% fibre‑based packaging9

    Energy

    • ENERGY STAR® certified10

    Smarter chemistry11
