# iPhone embedded-link audit — 6 November 2026

**Rules**
- `pending`: link not yet individually opened in this audit.
- `verified`: destination opened and its identity/relevance checked.
- `redirected`: destination resolves elsewhere; retain the original source text and document the canonical destination.
- `broken_or_mismatched`: destination fails or leads to unrelated content.
- Apple-hosted images and PDFs are included because they are linked evidence, not decorative extras.
- Source pages are already tracked in `reports/iphone-verification-register-2026-11-06.md`; this report covers links inside the source sections.
