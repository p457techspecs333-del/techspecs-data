    AirPods 5 Technical Specifications

    AirPods 5

    AirPods 5Wireless Charging Case

    Audio Technology1

    • Custom high‑excursion Apple driver
    • Custom high dynamic range amplifier
    • Active Noise Cancellation
    • Adaptive Audio2
    • Transparency
    • Conversation Awareness2
    • Voice Isolation2
    • Personalised Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking3
    • Adaptive EQ
    • Vent system for pressure equalisation

    Live Translation

    • Live Translation for communicating across languages4

    Sensors

    • Dual beam‑forming microphones
    • Inward‑facing microphone
    • Optical in‑ear sensor
    • Motion‑detecting accelerometer
    • Speech‑detecting accelerometer
    • Force sensor
    • Force sensor with volume swipe (AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case)

    Chip

    • H2 headphone chip

    Controls

    • Press once to play or pause media
    • Press once to answer a call or mute or unmute5
    • Press twice to end a call
    • Press twice to skip forward
    • Press three times to skip back
    • Press and hold to switch between listening modes
    • Swipe up or down to adjust volume (AirPods 5 with Wireless Char­ging Case)
    • Say “Hey Siri”6 to do things like play a song, make a call or get directions
    • With Siri Interactions, nod your head yes or gently shake your head no when Siri asks if you want to answer an incoming call, hear a text message, manage notifications and more2

    Dust, Sweat and Water Resistant7

    • Dust, sweat and water resistant (IP57)

    Charging Case

    AirPods 5

    • Works with USB‑C connector

    AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case

    • Works with USB‑C connector, Apple Watch charger or Qi‑certified chargers
    • Includes a speaker for use with Find My9

    Battery

    AirPods 5

    • Up to 4 hours of battery life on a single charge with Active Noise Cancellation enabled10
    • Up to 6 hours of battery life on a single charge with noise control off10

    Battery life with charging case

    • Up to 20 hours of battery life with Active Noise Cancellation enabled11
    • 5 minutes in the case provides around 1 hour of battery life12

    AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case

    • Up to 5 hours of battery life on a single charge with Active Noise Cancellation enabled13
    • Up to 7 hours of battery life on a single charge with noise control off13

    Battery life with charging case

    • Up to 22 hours of battery life with Active Noise Cancellation enabled14
    • 5 minutes in the case provides around 1 hour of battery life15

    • iPhone models with the latest version of iOS
    • iPad models with the latest version of iPadOS
    • Apple Watch models with the latest version of watchOS

    AirPods and the Environment

    See the AirPods 5 Product En­viron­men­tal Report (PDF)

    Progress towards Apple 2030

    Apple 2030 is our goal to be carbon neutral across our value chain. We design our products to be less carbon intensive by prioritising the use of recycled and renewable content and low‑carbon materials while focusing on the energy efficiency of our software and hardware.

    See Apple’s commitment

    Materials

    AirPods 5 are made with 40% recycled content,17 including:

    • 100% recycled aluminium in the hinge of the case
    • 100% recycled cobalt in the battery
    • 100% recycled gold plating and tin solder in all Apple‑designed printed circuit boards
    • 95% recycled lithium in the battery
    • 70% recycled plastic in the wireless charging case18
    • 100% recycled rare earth elements in all magnets

    Packaging

    • 100% fibre‑based packaging19

    Energy

    • 35% of manufacturing electricity for AirPods 5 is sourced from renewable electricity20
    • Exceeds US Department of Energy requirements for battery charger systems21

    Waste

    • No established final assembly sites generate waste sent to landfill as part of Apple’s Zero Waste Programme22

    Smarter Chemistry23

    All of the materials used in Apple products, accessories and packaging are covered by the requirements of our Regulated Substances Specification, which was one of the first in industry to restrict the use of key substances of concern. See the latest Environmental Progress Report to understand Apple’s recent efforts in phasing out these chemistries.
